      About VNRVJIET ScholarHub

      Institutional Scholarship Clearance & Digitally Signed Bonafide Portal

      What is VNRVJIET ScholarHub?

      VNRVJIET ScholarHub is the official digital scholarship gateway developed for students and administrators of Vallurupalli Nageswara Rao Vignana Jyothi Institute of Engineering and Technology (VNRVJIET), Hyderabad. The portal bridges the gap between students and external financial grant opportunities across state, national, and corporate domains.

      The platform enables students to discover verified external scholarships (such as TS ePASS, National Scholarship Portal - NSP, Reliance Foundation, Tata Trusts, and AICTE Pragati), verify their academic eligibility, and request official college permission. Upon administrative scrutiny, the college issues an authentic, Digitally Signed Bonafide Certificate that students can directly attach to their external scholarship applications.

      Key Portal Capabilities

      Centralized Scheme Directory

      Brings central, state, and corporate CSR scholarship opportunities into one curated window with real-time deadlines and award amounts.

      Automated Eligibility Check

      Instantly analyzes CGPA, family annual income, degree course, and category against official scheme cutoffs so students know exactly where they qualify.

      Digitally Signed Bonafides

      Eliminates physical paperwork by generating tamper-evident institutional certificates complete with college seal, digital signature, and verification hash.

      Transparent Status Pipeline

      Live 4-stage tracking from clearance submission to institutional endorsement, keeping students informed every step of the way.

      How It Works for Students

      1
      Explore External Scholarships

      Browse opportunities from TS ePASS, National Scholarship Portal (NSP), Reliance Foundation, Tata Trusts, and other government and corporate sponsors.

      2
      Request Institutional Clearance & Bonafide

      Select your scheme and submit a request with your official 10-digit VNRVJIET Roll Number (e.g. 23071A05XX) and branch details.

      3
      College Administrative Verification

      The VNRVJIET Scholarship Cell verifies your academic enrollment, attendance, and conduct records online.

      4
      Download Digitally Signed Bonafide & Apply Externally

      Download or print your official Digitally Signed Bonafide Certificate and upload it to the external sponsoring authority's portal to complete your grant application.
